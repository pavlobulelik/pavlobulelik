## Hi, I'm Pavlo 👋

Founder and CEO of [MedHarbor](https://medharborai.com), based in Lviv, Ukraine.

For nearly eight years I have worked where AI meets healthcare:

- **MedHarbor**: founder and CEO. Building an AI medical passport that turns a person's scattered records into one clear patient card, readable by any doctor in their own language.
- **Doctolib**: product for ecosystem and interoperability, on cross-border medical data exchange in Europe.
- **Grammarly**: product for NLP and generative AI.
- **SoftServe**: machine learning engineer, extracting data from scanned clinical documents for healthcare clients.

**Now:** MedHarbor is at the pre-seed stage. The concept and the patient card design are ready, and the next step is a first prototype built on real medical documents, supported by grant funding.

**Interests:** AI in healthcare, medical data interoperability, cross-border care.
